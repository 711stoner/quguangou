Know who doesn’t follow you back.

What it does

Two modes, one extension

Personal Account

Check current non-followers

Compare your signed-in X account’s Following and Followers lists. Review who you follow but who does not currently follow you back. This is not a historical unfollow tracker.

Community Blocklist

Review a shared account list

Browse a maintained community blocklist, then choose whether to process listed accounts. Submissions are reviewed; inclusion is not proof that an account has violated X rules.

1. Choose a mode

Start a personal scan or open the shared community list.

2. Review the accounts

Check the results and choose the accounts you want to act on.

3. Confirm an action

Blocks or unfollows are user-confirmed, paced one by one and subject to batch cooldowns.

Important: The extension does not identify who recently unfollowed you, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by X. Automated interactions may be restricted by X and could result in account limits or suspension. Review X’s terms and use the extension at your discretion.
