Two modes

One checks your relationships. The other shows a shared community list.

These modes serve different purposes and stay separate. Use Personal Account to check the accounts you follow, or Community Blocklist to explore a shared list before you engage.

Personal Account mode

Personal Account: Who do I follow that doesn't follow me back?

This mode analyzesyour own account. It compares your current Following and Followers lists to identify accounts you follow that do not currently follow you back.

  • Best for reviewing your own follow relationships
  • Shows current non-followers, not historical unfollows
  • You decide whether to block or unfollow after reviewing the list
Community Blocklist mode

Community Blocklist: Review accounts gathered from community reports

This modedoes not scan your private follow relationships. It presents a separate list based on public posts, follow-back discussions, voluntary submissions and manual review.

  • Best for checking community reports before following or engaging
  • You can open the shared list without running a personal scan
  • If you want to avoid an account, you can choose to block it

Why a shared list?

Review community reports before you follow

Personal Account showsyour own current relationships; Community Blocklist gathers public reports and user submissions in one place.

Before following back, engaging or deciding to follow someone, you can check the community list. If there's an account you would rather avoid, you canchoose to block it without having to interact with it first.

Community entries are for reference, not automatic judgments. You always decide and confirm whether to block.

Additional notes

Followback Watchdog is not affiliated with X. A non-follower result reflects current follow relationships only; it does not establish that someone previously unfollowed you.

The extension reads and interacts with X web pages, so interface updates and loading failures can affect its operation. Actions are paced deliberately, but X may still restrict automated activity.

Before using automated actions, review X's automation rules。
